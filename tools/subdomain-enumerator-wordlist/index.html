Most common subdomains found in the wild

Based on public scanning datasets (census-style scans and passive DNS corpora), these prefixes turn up most often on real domains. The highlighted ones appear on a large share of all scanned zones:

www is near-universal; mail, webmail, smtp, imap dominate mail infrastructure; api, app, dev, staging, test, demo are the most exposed dev/app hosts; vpn, remote, citrix, owa are common remote-access endpoints worth hardening. This list is a starting wordlist — for thorough subdomain enumeration, run a full wordlist (e.g. SecLists / subfinder / amass) with active resolution.
